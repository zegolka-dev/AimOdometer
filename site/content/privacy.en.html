Last updated: 5 October 2026. AimOdometer is a free, open-source Windows app; this page says exactly what it does with data. The source code is on GitHub, so every statement here can be checked.

On your PC

AimOdometer stores in %LOCALAPPDATA%\AimOdometer, per hour:

  • how far your mouse moved (raw counts and the mouse's DPI), clicks, wheel notches, time in motion and the fastest flick;
  • which mouse it was (its Windows device id and the name you give it);
  • which program was in front (the path of its .exe) and for how long, to sort distance by game;
  • your settings, achievements and gear.

Never read or stored: the keyboard, the screen, window titles, what you type or do in games, game memory, files.

The background tracker never uses the internet.

If you sign in through Steam

Signing in is optional. Steam tells AimOdometer your SteamID; AimOdometer never sees your Steam password. The cloud then stores:

  • your SteamID, Steam name, avatar link, profile link and country (public Steam profile data);
  • daily totals per game and per mouse: distance, clicks, time in motion, fastest flick and the DPI they were measured at. Mice are identified by a one-way hash, not their hardware id;
  • for each PC: a random id, the PC's name and the last sync time;
  • your Steam friend list (SteamIDs only), cached for an hour, to find friends who use AimOdometer.

Who sees you: Steam friends who use AimOdometer see your name, avatar, distance, the DPI behind your distance and fastest flick, and fair-play badges such as “Clown” for distance inflated by a DPI set far too low (on by default, can be switched off). The world leaderboard shows the same to everyone, only if you turn it on. Nobody else can read your data: every table is protected by row level security and tested.

Only the window syncs: when it opens, every 15 minutes while it is open, and when it closes.

Complaints and suggestions

When you send a message from “Complaints and suggestions”, it is stored in the AimOdometer cloud with its type, the contact you typed (optional), the app version and Windows version (can be unticked), the app language and, if you are signed in, your Steam name. Only the author reads these messages.

Deleting your data

  • Cloud: Settings › Steam account and cloud › “Delete cloud account” deletes the account and everything stored for it, at once.
  • This PC: Settings › Your data › “Delete statistics on this PC”. Uninstalling AimOdometer keeps statistics unless you delete them.

No tracking, no ads

AimOdometer has no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and sells nothing. This website uses no cookies and no third-party analytics; it loads fonts from Google Fonts and asks GitHub for the newest version to show on the download button. When you click a download button, the site tells our own server which button it was, the page language and the site you came from (just its name, such as youtube.com), so the author can see how many people download the app. Your IP address is not stored: the server keeps only a code made from it that changes every day, to count one person once. Nothing is saved in your browser.
